# Release 21.19.2 — Attendance Correction, Historical OT, and Overnight Safeguards

- Freelancers may request corrections for previous attendance dates with a reason.
- Administrators approve/reject requests, and may continue correcting records directly without a request.
- Administrators may enter and immediately approve verified historical overtime.
- From midnight to 06:00 local time, open attendance/Work Orders remain active under the assumption work continues.
- At 06:00 local time, unresolved prior-day attendance and Work Orders are flagged; OT is unavailable until Administrator correction.
- The safeguard records an audit trail and does not silently grant overtime credit.
- English and Traditional Chinese UI are included.
