Current calculation rules

  • Arrival within the grace period has zero late minutes.
  • After the grace period, late minutes count from scheduled start.
  • Undertime is the difference between Time Out and scheduled end.
  • Time after scheduled end is potential overtime, not yet approved.
  • Break is deducted only after the configured elapsed-time trigger.
  • All rendered time on a rest day is treated as potential rest-day work.
Default 300 means a 60-minute break applies after 5 hours.
